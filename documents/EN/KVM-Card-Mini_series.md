# KVM-Card-Mini Series

## Introduction

KVM-Card-Mini is open-source hardware designed by [Jackadminx](https://github.com/Jackadminx).

For detailed information about this hardware, please refer to: [KVM-Card-Mini](https://github.com/Jackadminx/KVM-Card-Mini)

## How to Obtain KVM-Card-Mini Hardware

If you cannot manufacture and solder the PCB yourself, it is recommended to obtain KVM-Card-Mini hardware through the following two channels:

1. Fabrication services are currently offered on the Xianyu app. You can search for the relevant keywords and contact a seller to purchase one.

2. Buy a Sunflower Q0.5 (向日葵Q0.5) and flash it with the KVM-Card-Mini firmware.

For detailed information, please refer to:

[KVM_over_USB_Q05_from_binnehot](https://github.com/binnehot/KVM_over_USB_Q05)

[KVM_over_USB_Q05_from_do21](https://github.com/do21/KVM_over_USB_Q05)

## Obtaining Firmware

Note: The firmware provided by the [KVM-Card-Mini](https://github.com/Jackadminx/KVM-Card-Mini) project does not support the relative mouse function.

It is recommended to use the firmware provided by [ElluIFX](https://github.com/ElluIFX).
Project address: [KVM-Card-Mini-PySide6](https://github.com/ElluIFX/KVM-Card-Mini-PySide6)
Alternatively, use the firmware provided by this project: [KVM-Card-Mini-Firmware.hex](https://github.com/wevsty/KVM-over-USB/releases/download/v2026.09.30/KVM-Card-Mini-Firmware.hex)

## Flashing Firmware

To flash the firmware, please refer to the [KVM-Card-Mini documentation](https://github.com/Jackadminx/KVM-Card-Mini#%E5%9B%BA%E4%BB%B6%E5%88%B7%E5%85%A5).
